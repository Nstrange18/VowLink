# VowLink Video Ad Workflow

Reusable workflow for creating short promotional videos for VowLink, a digital wedding invitation, RSVP, guest management, and event check-in platform.

## Brand Direction

**Style:** Glossy, romantic, premium, modern, wedding-tech, Nigerian wedding-friendly.

**Core promise:** VowLink turns a wedding invitation into a beautiful interactive guest experience.

**Audience:** Engaged couples, wedding planners, family coordinators, venue owners, and guests who mostly receive invites through WhatsApp.

**Visual world:** Champagne gold, ivory, navy, soft florals, premium phone mockups, elegant Nigerian wedding details, RSVP screens, guest lists, WhatsApp sharing, QR check-in, and happy guests entering a hall.

**Avoid:** Cheap template look, cluttered screens, fake app names, unreadable tiny text, unrealistic claims, hard pricing unless current pricing is confirmed.

## 15-Second Ad Script

**Concept:** From WhatsApp chaos to one beautiful wedding link.

**0-3s**  
Visual: Couple looking at many scattered WhatsApp messages and guest questions.  
Overlay: `Still sending wedding details one by one?`  
Voiceover: "Planning a wedding and tired of repeating details?"

**3-7s**  
Visual: Phone shows a VowLink digital invite opening beautifully.  
Overlay: `Send one beautiful invite link`  
Voiceover: "With VowLink, every guest gets a beautiful digital invitation."

**7-11s**  
Visual: RSVP dashboard, WhatsApp share, guest list, QR check-in.  
Overlay: `RSVPs. WhatsApp. Guest check-in.`  
Voiceover: "Track RSVPs, share on WhatsApp, and check guests in with QR codes."

**11-15s**  
Visual: Premium VowLink logo, invite card, smiling couple.  
Overlay: `Launch your wedding link`  
Voiceover: "VowLink. Your wedding invitation, online."

## 30-Second Ad Script

**Concept:** A complete wedding guest experience.

**0-4s**  
Visual: Bride/groom or planner scrolling through messages, guest list papers, and reminders.  
Overlay: `Wedding planning gets messy fast.`  
Voiceover: "Between guest names, RSVPs, directions, dress codes, and reminders, wedding planning can get stressful."

**4-9s**  
Visual: VowLink invite opens on a phone with elegant wedding design.  
Overlay: `Create a digital invitation`  
Voiceover: "VowLink gives your wedding a beautiful digital invitation page."

**9-14s**  
Visual: Guest taps RSVP, selects attendance and meal preference.  
Overlay: `Guests RSVP online`  
Voiceover: "Guests can RSVP online, so you know who is coming before the big day."

**14-19s**  
Visual: Admin dashboard shows attending, not attending, no response, bride/groom invite split.  
Overlay: `Track everything in one dashboard`  
Voiceover: "Your dashboard keeps invitations, responses, and guest groups organized."

**19-24s**  
Visual: WhatsApp share button, personalized invite link, QR code entry.  
Overlay: `Share on WhatsApp. Check in with QR.`  
Voiceover: "Share invites through WhatsApp and use QR check-in at the entrance."

**24-30s**  
Visual: Wedding hall entrance, guests entering smoothly, VowLink logo.  
Overlay: `VowLink — launch your wedding link`  
Voiceover: "Make your wedding feel premium from the first invite. Launch your VowLink."

## Recommended Music/Mood

**Mood:** Romantic, refined, hopeful, modern, lightly celebratory.

**Music direction:**

- Soft Afrobeats-inspired instrumental with wedding warmth
- Piano and strings with gentle percussion
- Premium lifestyle ad music with a rising finish
- Avoid overly loud club beats or sad cinematic music

**Pacing:**

- 15s ad: quick cuts every 2-4 seconds
- 30s ad: smoother story, 4-5 seconds per scene

## Export Specs

### Instagram Reels

- Aspect ratio: `9:16`
- Resolution: `1080x1920`
- Duration: `15s` or `30s`
- Format: `MP4`
- Codec: `H.264`
- FPS: `30`
- Audio: AAC, 44.1kHz or 48kHz
- Keep text inside safe margins: at least `120px` from top and bottom

### TikTok

- Aspect ratio: `9:16`
- Resolution: `1080x1920`
- Duration: `15s` or `30s`
- Format: `MP4`
- Codec: `H.264`
- FPS: `30`
- Use larger text overlays because TikTok UI covers screen edges

### WhatsApp Status

- Aspect ratio: `9:16`
- Resolution: `1080x1920`
- Duration: ideally `15s`, max `30s`
- Format: `MP4`
- Codec: `H.264`
- Keep file size reasonable, preferably under `15MB`
- Use very readable text because many viewers watch quickly

## FFmpeg Workflow

Use these only when you already have images/video clips and audio.

### Create 15s Video From 5 Images

```bash
ffmpeg -y \
  -loop 1 -t 3 -i scene1.png \
  -loop 1 -t 3 -i scene2.png \
  -loop 1 -t 3 -i scene3.png \
  -loop 1 -t 3 -i scene4.png \
  -loop 1 -t 3 -i scene5.png \
  -filter_complex "[0:v]scale=1080:1920:force_original_aspect_ratio=increase,crop=1080:1920,setsar=1[v0];[1:v]scale=1080:1920:force_original_aspect_ratio=increase,crop=1080:1920,setsar=1[v1];[2:v]scale=1080:1920:force_original_aspect_ratio=increase,crop=1080:1920,setsar=1[v2];[3:v]scale=1080:1920:force_original_aspect_ratio=increase,crop=1080:1920,setsar=1[v3];[4:v]scale=1080:1920:force_original_aspect_ratio=increase,crop=1080:1920,setsar=1[v4];[v0][v1][v2][v3][v4]concat=n=5:v=1:a=0,format=yuv420p[v]" \
  -map "[v]" -r 30 -c:v libx264 -pix_fmt yuv420p vowlink-15s-ad.mp4
```

### Add Music

```bash
ffmpeg -y -i vowlink-15s-ad.mp4 -i music.mp3 \
  -shortest -c:v copy -c:a aac -b:a 192k vowlink-15s-ad-with-music.mp4
```

### Add Simple Text Overlay

```bash
ffmpeg -y -i input.mp4 \
  -vf "drawtext=text='Launch your wedding link':fontcolor=white:fontsize=54:x=(w-text_w)/2:y=h-260:box=1:boxcolor=black@0.35:boxborderw=24" \
  -c:a copy output-with-text.mp4
```

### Compress For WhatsApp

```bash
ffmpeg -y -i input.mp4 \
  -vf "scale=1080:1920:force_original_aspect_ratio=decrease,pad=1080:1920:(ow-iw)/2:(oh-ih)/2" \
  -c:v libx264 -crf 28 -preset medium -c:a aac -b:a 128k vowlink-whatsapp-status.mp4
```

## Quick Production Checklist

1. Choose `15s` or `30s`.
2. Generate or collect 5-7 scene assets.
3. Add large text overlays.
4. Record voiceover or use text-to-speech.
5. Add soft romantic music.
6. Export vertical `1080x1920`.
7. Watch once on a phone before posting.
8. Post with a clear caption and call-to-action.
